# evidence/

Everything your proposal cites or rests on. Claude may cite only what is here.
When it needs something that isn't, it says "not in evidence/" rather than
filling the gap from memory.

| File | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `cv.md` | Your CV, in any readable format |
| `products.md` | The fixed list of your products (papers, preprints, datasets, software) the biosketch skill may choose from, each with its full citation and a PMID, PMCID or DOI |
| `references.md` | Every paper your drafts cite, one entry each, with a PMID or DOI. A `.bib` or `.ris` export from Zotero or EndNote works too |
| PDFs | The full text of the papers your argument leans on, named by first author and year, such as `Chuang2007.pdf` |
| Preliminary data | The figures or tables behind the numbers in your drafts, each with a line on what it shows and where it came from |

The citation list is the minimum. It is enough to show that each paper exists.
Whether a paper supports the sentence citing it is a harder question, and
without the full text `reference-check` can often only answer "cannot tell from
the abstract". Add PDFs for the papers that carry the most weight.

Unpublished data is confidential. Keep this folder on your Scripps account.
