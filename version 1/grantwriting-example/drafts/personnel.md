**Andrew Su, Ph.D., Principal Investigator (effort = 1.2 calendar months):** Dr. Su is an Associate Professor at the Scripps Research Institute (TSRI) in the Department of Molecular and Experimental Medicine. He leads a group of seven people. He has authored 50 publications in bioinformatics and genomics that in total have been cited more than 7000 times. His group develops bioinformatics tools to warehouse, analyze and visualize genomic data, and also applies those tools for biomedical discovery. He is co-Executive Editor of the journal Gene and a Section Editor for BMC Genomics. He is currently the PI on two R01 grants, and he will serve as the PI on this proposal as well.

**Benjamin Good, Ph. D., Co-Investigator (effort = 10 calendar months)** will be responsible for the design, implementation and evaluation of both the game prototypes and the algorithms required for making use of the data collected from the games. Dr. Good has authored 36 peer-reviewed articles in bioinformatics. He has core bioinformatics research skills in data analysis and experimental design as well as professional experience in the development of interactive Web and client-server software applications
