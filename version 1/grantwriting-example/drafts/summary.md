The central hypothesis is that 'serious games' can be developed that attract large numbers of biologist players that, through the actions they take in these games, contribute the knowledge needed to build state of the art predictors of breast cancer progression. In addition to generating more accurate predictors, the proposed work will produce a reusable framework for capturing and applying community intelligence in biology.
