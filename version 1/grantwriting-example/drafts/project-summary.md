# Project Summary/Abstract

Predictive models of the progression of breast cancer and other complex disease based on genomic data could be powerful tools in the clinic and in the laboratory yet, in practice, they remain rare. The long-term goal is to develop a complete system for accurately inferring such models. The overall objective of this application is to ascertain how the diverse human insight needed to achieve the long-term goal can be rapidly and effectively captured and integrated. The central hypothesis is that 'serious games' can be developed that attract large numbers of biologist players that, through the actions they take in these games, contribute the knowledge needed to build state of the art predictors of breast cancer progression. The rationale that underlies the proposed research is that the output of this project will make it possible to rapidly capture diverse biological knowledge that will improve the inference of predictive models. In addition to generating more accurate predictors, the proposed work will produce a reusable framework for capturing and applying community intelligence in biology. Such results are expected to have an important positive impact on health by supporting new predictive tests for breast cancer progression with increased accuracy. The specific aims of this proposal are (1) to implement a serious game for building predictors of breast cancer progression and (2) to create a computational framework that integrates data from games with data from biological experiments to infer genomic predictors of breast cancer progression. These aims will be accomplished through the development of a Web-based game and the design and implementation of a suite of algorithms for filtering and integrating the knowledge output of the game. This research will generate a novel crowdsourcing system that provides a mechanism to tap into massive, unused reservoirs of biological knowledge and human intuition. In doing so, this system has the potential to make major, disruptive improvements to the prediction of breast cancer progression based on genomic data. In addition, the methodology developed here will be applicable to analogous problems in many other disease areas. The proposed research is innovative, in our opinion, because it alters the process of assembling genomic predictors informed by community knowledge from a passive one that is entirely dependent on the growth of external, general purpose databases to an active one that offers the potential to rapidly generate focused knowledge bases oriented specifically around solving particular biological problems.

# Project Narrative (Public Health Relevance Statement)

The proposed research is relative to public health because it stands to substantially improve the accuracy of predictions of breast cancer progression based on genomic measurements from clinical samples. Such predictions can be used to improve patient care by giving doctors much more information about the state of this disease in a given patient. This research would also yield a methodology that should make similar advances possible for many other diseases.
