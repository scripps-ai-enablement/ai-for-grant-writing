# Preliminary data

The Preliminary Data in `drafts/approach.md` come from the authors' own analysis
of play in the prototype game, The Cure (product P16), in September 2012. The
figures as submitted are in `drafts/figures/`. The game logs, the registration
survey and the analysis behind the numbers are not in this folder.

| Claim in the draft | Source recorded |
|---|---|
| More than 120 players registered and played more than 2000 games, 7-14 September 2012 | Game logs; not in this folder |
| Nearly half of players self-reported PhDs; half claimed knowledge of cancer biology | Registration survey; not in this folder |
| Top 10 genes gave decision tree predictors of 10-year survival with 65% accuracy in 10-fold cross-validation, against 55% for random genes | Authors' analysis; the dataset is not named in the draft; not in this folder |
| 10 gene combinations gave significant (FDR < 0.05) survival indicators in the training set, a validation cohort and an independent set | Authors' analysis; the three datasets are not named in the draft; not in this folder |
| The predictor submitted to the SAGE Breast Cancer Prognosis challenge scored 69% on concordance index, 3% behind the best | Challenge results page (http://tinyurl.com/sage-bcc); not in this folder |
