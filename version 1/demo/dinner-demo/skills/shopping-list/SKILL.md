# Shopping list

1. List every ingredient, with quantities for two.
2. Anything in pantry.md goes under "Already have", not on the list.
3. Group the rest by section: Produce, Dairy and eggs, Grains and cans, Spices, Other.
4. No brands and no prices.
5. Check: every ingredient appears exactly once. Say whether the check passed.
